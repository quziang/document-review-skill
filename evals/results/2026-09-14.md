## Limits and next checks

In `equivalent-wording`, the checkpoint omitted the application's untested state, so that criterion remains unobserved. In `explicit-batch`, the assistant labeled the format as recognition, but grading future submitted choices was not exercised.

Observed behavior included cued success remaining partial, answer echoes not becoming independent evidence, source ambiguity being repaired, level-specific states, eligible revisit selection, positive spaced confirmation, and resetting confirmation after failure. No live long-session state-retention run, real-time budget test, unreadable-file tool recovery, cross-model comparison, or learner-retention experiment was conducted. These remain separate validation work.
